Engineering Whitepaper: Architecture & Intellectual Honesty

1. Regulatory Framing & UCC Compliance Mandate

Under the Uganda Communications Commission (UCC) Quality of Service Regulations, licensed mobile network operators (including MTN Uganda, Airtel Uganda, and Uganda Telecom) are legally mandated to maintain a Call Drop Rate (CDR) strictly below 2.0% and a Call Setup Success Rate above 95%. PulseUganda functions as an automated data lakehouse engine engineered to ingest high-velocity telemetry, geographically match signals to licensed cell sites, and isolate equipment SLA breaches from environmental disruptions.

2. Intellectual Honesty & Synthetic CDR Physics

To earn credibility with senior engineering leadership, this platform explicitly details the technical boundaries of its telemetry:

  • The Legal Constraint (Uganda Data Protection Act 2019): Production telecommunications Call Detail Records contain sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) including subscriber MSISDNs, hardware IMEIs, and cell tower handovers. Under Ugandan law and international GDPR statutes, raw production CDRs cannot be publicly distributed.
  • The Engineering Integrity: Our generator does not produce flat random numbers. It simulates 3GPP and GSMA radio propagation physics: log-normal signal degradation (RSRP $[-140, -40]\text{ dBm}$), distance-based path loss, Poisson call arrivals, and realistic drop-call probabilities correlating with WMO atmospheric thunderstorm severity.
  • Ground Truth Infrastructure: Uses real crowd-sourced OpenCellID telemetry for Uganda (MCC 641, 33,051 masts) enriched with boundary polygons from the UN-OCHA / HDX spatial dataset across 5 administrative layers (admin0 to admin4).
⚡ Microwave Rain Fade Physics (15–23 GHz): In developing markets like Uganda, over 80% of cell sites rely on microwave radio dishes rather than fiber optic backhaul. Equatorial rainstorms produce raindrops whose physical diameters match microwave wavelengths, causing severe signal scattering and attenuation (Rain Fade). Ingesting district weather telemetry from Open-Meteo allows the platform to prove whether an SLA breach was caused by an environmental storm or operator network negligence.

3. The $0 Local Production Lakehouse Stack

Adhering to the standard pipeline_blueprint, the platform executes on a commodity laptop for $0 in monthly compute bills while remaining 100% API-compatible with enterprise cloud architectures (AWS / Databricks):

  • Redpanda (Kafka): Single C++ binary broker running on 512MB RAM without JVM overhead.
  • RustFS (S3): High-throughput, Rust-based object store replacing archived MinIO CE.
  • Delta-RS: Rust-native Delta Lake ACID transaction logs, micro-batch CDC compaction, and Time Travel without Spark.
  • DuckDB + dbt: In-process vectorized columnar analytics compiling Kimball dimensional models with 18 automated data tests.
  • Dagster: Declarative Software-Defined Assets and S3 storage sensors replacing fragile Cron timers.
